# Realty 2.1.1

Fixes a late offer payment being accepted after its deadline, and an auction going back to a bidder who had already missed their payment.

## Upgrade

Replace the Realty plugin jar. The database schema is unchanged.

## Fixes

- **A late offer payment is refused.** `/realty offer pay` and `RealtyPaperApi.payOffer` accepted a payment after the offer's payment deadline, until the periodic sweep refunded it. That payment could complete the purchase. It is now refused before any money is taken, as an auction payment already was. The new message is `pay-offer.payment-expired` in `messages.yml`.
- **A missed auction payment moves on in bid order.** When a winner missed their payment, the plot went to the highest bid from anyone else. If that runner-up missed their payment too, the plot went back to the first bidder, and the two alternated without ever reaching the third. Each missed payment now passes to the next bidder down, skipping everyone who has already had a turn.

## For plugin developers

- **`PayOfferResult` gains `PaymentExpired`**, in `RealtyPaperApi` and in `RealtyBackend`, matching `PayBidResult.PaymentExpired`. An exhaustive `switch` over `PayOfferResult` must handle it. Code compiled against 2.1.0 throws `MatchException` when it meets one.
- **`listActiveAuctions()` reports the bid being paid for.** While a runner-up is paying, `ActiveAuction.highestBid()` is now their bid. Before, it was the defaulter's higher bid, which named the wrong bidder and amount.
