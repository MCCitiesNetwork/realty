# Realty 2.3.0

A freehold sale can now carry fees: levies the server owner sets in `fees.yml`, and fees that players with a role on a plot attach to it. Auction and offer instalments now wait in a holding account until the sale finishes, so a buyer whose payment deadline passes gets their money back. This release changes the database, so the plugin and `realty-rest` must be upgraded together.

## Upgrade

`realty-rest` 2.3.0 refuses to start against a database schema other than version 21. The plugin moves the database to version 21 the first time it starts.

1. **On a Vault-only server, choose an escrow player first.** Vault can only move money between players, so instalments need a player account to wait in. Add `escrow: { name: <player> }` to `settings.yml`, next to the default parties. Until you do, no auction can move from bidding to payment and no offer can be accepted. Bidding and placing offers still work. A Treasury server needs nothing: Realty creates its own account.
2. **On a Treasury server, run a current Treasury.** Realty 2.3.0 is built against Treasury API `2026.11.38.1`.
3. Stop `realty-rest`.
4. Replace the Realty plugin jar with its 2.3.0 version and restart the game server. When the migration has finished, the log shows `Applied migration V21: sale fees`. On Treasury, the first start also logs `Created the Treasury account 'Realty Holding'`.
5. Replace `realty-rest` with its 2.3.0 version and start it. Do this only after step 4.
6. If you use the PlayerNotifications module, replace it with its 2.3.0 version, so the new notifications sort into the right categories. The other modules can stay on 2.0.

No fee is charged until you set one up. The shipped levy is turned off.

### Payments already in progress

A payment that began before the upgrade finishes under the old rules. Its instalments go to the seller, it carries no fees, and if its deadline passes nothing is refunded, because the seller already has the money. The buyer is told so.

### Check `messages.yml`

New message keys are added to your `messages.yml` automatically. Keys you already have are left alone, so these keep their old text until you replace them with the text in `plugins/Realty/defaults/default-messages.yml`:

- `pay-bid.success` and `pay-offer.success` now show the total owed, fees included, and the deadline.
- `buy.insufficient-funds` now says the price includes fees.
- `notification.auction-won` and `notification.offer-accepted` now show what the buyer owes and by when.
- The lists under `help` do not show the new `fee` and `levy` commands.

`notification.bid-payment-expired` and `notification.offer-payment-expired` are no longer used, so you can delete them. A missed bid deadline now sends one of four notices, depending on the refund:

| Key | When |
|---|---|
| `notification.bid-payment-expired-refunded` | The refund was paid |
| `notification.bid-refund-pending` | The refund failed and will be retried |
| `notification.bid-payment-expired-nothing-paid` | The buyer had paid nothing |
| `notification.bid-payment-expired-old-rules` | The payment began before 2.3.0, so nothing is refunded |

A missed offer deadline sends the same four with `offer` in place of `bid`.

## Sale fees

A fee is a percentage of the sale price, paid to someone other than the seller. Each fee is paid either by the buyer, on top of the price, or by the seller, out of the price. Fees apply to every freehold sale: auctions, offers and `/realty buy`.

### Levies, in `fees.yml`

The server owner defines one or more named levies in the new file `fees.yml`, for example a national levy and one per town. Each levy has rules matched against the plot's tags, as in `taxes.yml`, and tiers that raise the rate with the number of plots the buyer already holds personally.

- Each plot pays at most one levy: the plot's own choice, else its authority's choice, else the levy marked `default`.
- An authority's managers choose the levy with `/realty levy set <levy> [region] --authority`, or for all their plots with `--all`. `/realty levy clear` undoes a choice. Only the server owner sets the rates.
- `/realty levy list` shows every levy and its rates.
- At most one levy may be marked `default`. With more than one, Realty logs an error and loads no levies.
- A choice that names a levy no longer in `fees.yml` is ignored, and Realty logs a warning at startup.

### Fees players attach

Anyone with a role on a freehold, its authority's managers, its titleholder or an agent, can attach their own fee with `/realty fee set <percent> <buyer|seller> [region] [--authority]`. The fee is paid to whoever set it.

- A seller-paid fee set by someone other than the seller waits for the seller's approval: `/realty fee approve` or `/realty fee reject`.
- An agent's fee is paid only on a sale that agent ran.
- `/realty fee list [region]` shows every fee on a plot. `/realty info` shows them too.

### What buyers see

- Every fee is shown before a bid, an offer or a buy.
- `/realty buy` asks for confirmation when any fee applies. If the total changes before the buyer confirms, nothing is bought and the new total is shown.
- Fees are fixed when the sale locks: at a buy, when bidding ends, at a handover to the next bidder, or when an offer is accepted. Later changes do not affect a locked sale.
- While an auction runs, in bidding or in payment, or while an offer is open, nobody can raise a buyer-paid fee on the plot or change its levy. An authority-wide levy change leaves those plots on the levy they had.

## Changes in behaviour

- **Instalments wait in a holding account.** Each auction or offer instalment is paid from the buyer into the holding account. When the last one arrives, the holding account pays the seller the price minus seller-paid fees, and pays each fee to its recipient. `/realty buy` pays everyone directly, as before.
- **A missed deadline refunds the buyer.** Before, the buyer was told they had been refunded, but no money moved. Now the holding account pays back everything they paid. A refund that fails is recorded and retried, and the buyer is told it is pending.
- **A failed payout does not hold up the buyer.** Once the buyer has paid in full, the title is theirs even if paying the seller or a fee fails. The failed payout is retried every scheduler run, up to 24 times.
- **Payments pause while the holding account is unusable.** If the account is archived, or no escrow player is set, Realty refuses instalments, does not move auctions to payment or accept offers, and keeps pushing deadlines back so no buyer is expired by it. Admins see one error in the log when it stops working and one notice when it recovers.
- **The title cannot change hands during a sale.** `/realty transfer` and `/realty set titleholder` are refused while a buyer is paying for the plot.
- **Bids are refused once bidding has closed**, while the auction waits to move to payment.
- **Auction money carries its own source in Treasury.** Bid instalments and an auction seller's payout from the holding account record `plugin_system=realty-auction`. Fee payouts, refunds and every other payment keep `realty`.
- **Auctions completed before 2.3.0 are marked ended.** The migration marks every auction whose plot was already sold at auction as ended, so the scheduler no longer settles it a second time.

### New permissions

All default to `true` except the bypass. Realty still checks that the player has the right role.

| Permission | Command |
|---|---|
| `realty.command.fee.set`, `.remove`, `.list`, `.approve`, `.reject` | `/realty fee ...` |
| `realty.command.levy.set`, `.clear`, `.list` | `/realty levy ...` |
| `realty.command.levy.bypass` (op) | Set or clear a levy on any plot or for any authority |

## For admins: payments that need a hand

Realty logs these at `SEVERE`. Nothing is lost: each record stays in the database.

- **A payout or refund that failed 24 times** is not tried again. The log names it. Pay it by hand from the holding account.
- **A payout or refund that was being paid when the server stopped** is listed at the next start. Whether its money moved is unknown, so check each in the economy's ledger. They are not retried automatically.
- **An instalment pending for more than 10 minutes**, as after a crash mid-payment, is logged with what to change in `FreeholdContractBidPayment` or `FreeholdContractOfferPayment`. Its deadline is suspended until an admin clears it.
- **On Vault, changing the escrow player** while sales hold money logs a warning. The money stays with the old escrow player until you move it.

## For plugin developers

This covers `realty-paper-api` and `realty-backend-api`.

- **Records gained fields, so code that builds or deconstructs them must be rebuilt.** `BuyResult.Success`, `PayBidResult.FullyPaid` and `PayOfferResult.FullyPaid` gain a `FeeQuote`. `ActiveAuction` and `OfferListing` gain a nullable `FeeQuote`, set once the sale has locked. `AcceptOfferResult.Success` gains the price, the total due and the payment deadline.
- **Sealed results gained variants.** An exhaustive `switch` must handle them. Code compiled against 2.2 throws `MatchException` when it meets one.
  - `PayBidResult` and `PayOfferResult`: `FinalizePending`, `InvalidAmount`, `HoldingPaused`.
  - `SetTitleHolderResult`: `SaleInPayment`.
  - `BidResult`: `BiddingClosed`.
  - `AcceptOfferResult`: `NoHoldingAccount`.
- **`PayBidResult.Success` and `PayOfferResult.Success`** report amounts against the total the buyer owes, fees included.
- **New methods on `RealtyPaperApi`:** `previewFees(region, buyerId, price)`, which saves nothing; `setFee`, `removeFee`, `approveFee`, `rejectFee` and `listFees`; `setRegionLevy`, `clearRegionLevy`, `setAuthorityLevy`, `clearAuthorityLevy` and `listLevies`.
- **New types:** `FeeQuote`, `FeeLine`, `FeeSide`, `LevyDefinition`, `LevyRule`, `LevyTier` and `Money`.
- **Changed events:** `RegionBoughtEvent`, `AuctionWonPurchaseEvent` and `OfferPurchaseCompletedEvent` gain `getFees()`. `OfferAcceptedEvent` gains `getPrice()`, `getTotalDue()` and `getPaymentDeadline()`. `PaymentExpiredEvent` gains `isRefundPaid()` and `isOldRules()`. Their constructors take the new fields.
- **New events:** `FeeChangeEvent` (cancellable) and `FeeChangedEvent`; `FeeRemovedEvent`, `FeeApprovedEvent` and `FeeRejectedEvent`; `LevyChangeEvent` (cancellable) and `LevyChangedEvent`; `FeePaidEvent`, `SaleProceedsPaidEvent` and `RefundPaidEvent`, which also fire when a retry succeeds.
