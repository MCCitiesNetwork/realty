# Sale Fees Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Freehold sales carry fees (levies from `fees.yml` and player fees), and auction and offer instalments wait in a holding account until the sale finishes, so an expired payment is refunded.

**Architecture:** The backend works out and locks fee lines, because only it sees the region, its roles and the buyer's holdings inside the sale's transaction. It reads the levies through a `LevyCatalog` interface that the Paper plugin implements from `fees.yml`. The Paper plugin moves the money. Instalments go to a holding party, and a new `SaleSettlement` pays out locked lines and refunds from it, recording every failure in the database for the scheduler to retry.

**Tech Stack:** Java 25, Gradle, Paper, MyBatis annotation mappers on MariaDB, Configurate, Cloud commands, JUnit 5 with Testcontainers (backend) and Mockito (paper).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-sale-fees-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The release is 2.3.0, delivered as one gh stack. Each PR has one plain paragraph and ends with the attribution line. Every commit ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- There is one migration, `V21__sale_fees.sql`. `SchemaVersionCheck.EXPECTED_VERSION` becomes `21`.
- Each fee rounds down to the cent, and the seller receives the remainder: `sellerReceives = price - sum(seller lines)`, `buyerTotal = price + sum(buyer lines)`.
- A fee whose recipient is the buyer is left out.
- A plot's levy is the plot's own choice, then its authority's choice, then the levy marked `default`. At most one levy applies to a sale.
- The plot count is the number of titles the buyer holds personally, not counting the region being bought.
- At most one levy may be `default`. A `fees.yml` with more than one is refused, and no levy then applies.
- The holding party is Realty's Treasury SYSTEM account, owned by `UUID.nameUUIDFromBytes("realty:holding".getBytes(UTF_8))` and named `Realty Holding`, with overdraft off. On Vault it is the player named by `escrow` in `settings.yml`.
- Payments made before 2.3.0 (`oldRules = TRUE`) pay the seller directly, carry no fees and get no refund.
- Failed payouts and refunds are retried by the minute scheduler at most once an hour, up to 24 attempts. After the last attempt, Realty logs SEVERE and the row stays.
- Money is formatted with `CurrencyFormatter.format`, as elsewhere.
- No personal paths, usernames or real names appear in committed text.

## Decisions this plan adds to the spec

The spec leaves these open. Each is fixed here and pinned by a test.

1. **Seller fees can't exceed the price.** Seller-paid lines are applied in order: the levy first, then player fees by creation time. Each is cut down to whatever is left of the price, so the seller never receives a negative amount. Buyer-paid lines have no cap.
2. **Role changes clean up fees.** When the title changes hands (buy, auction or offer completion, `setTitleHolder`, `transferTitleHolder`), the previous titleholder's fees are deleted. Seller-paid fees set by anyone else revert to unapproved. When an agent is removed, or all agents are cleared at completion, their fees are deleted.
3. **An agent fee knows it is one.** `RegionFee.role` records the role used when the fee was set: `authority`, `titleholder` or `agent`. An `agent` fee applies only when the sale's `ranBy` equals the fee's `setBy`.
4. **The seller's proceeds are a payout line too.** `SaleFee` holds the fee lines and one `proceeds` line (`lineKind`), so a failed payout to the seller is retried the same way as a fee.
5. **A deleted region still refunds.** Deleting a region whose payment is in progress under the new rules records a `PendingRefund` of what the buyer has paid. `SaleFee` and `PendingRefund` keep the region's name and world, and their region id is set to null rather than the rows being deleted.
6. **`/realty buy` pays directly.** It pays the seller, then each fee recipient. If any transfer fails, Realty reverses the ones already made and rolls back the reservation. A reversal that fails is logged SEVERE.
7. **An auction without a holding account waits.** On Vault with no `escrow` named, an auction whose bidding has ended stays unsettled. The scheduler leaves it alone and logs one warning per auction per startup. Accepting an offer returns `AcceptOfferResult.NoHoldingAccount`.

## Review Focus

Most likely to bite first:

1. **A payout fails halfway through completion.** The seller is paid and a fee recipient's account is archived. The buyer still owns the plot, the seller's line is `paid`, and the fee line is `failed` and retried. Pinned in Task 7.
2. **The server restarts between `finalize*` and the payouts.** Lines left `locked` on a completed sale are paid by the next scheduler run, and none is paid twice. Pinned in Task 7.
3. **A runner-up takes over the auction.** The fees are worked out again from the new buyer's plot count, and the defaulter's lines are deleted. Pinned in Task 6.
4. **Seller-paid fees add up to more than 100%.** The seller receives 0 and the lines are cut down. Pinned in Task 2.
5. **The region is deleted while instalments are held.** The buyer gets a `PendingRefund`. Pinned in Task 6.

    - The levy goes through the fallback, and is dropped when `authorityKind` doesn't match. The authority's kind is `PERSONAL` for a player, `PartyKind.of(kind)` for an account, and `PartyKind.of(accountKind)` for a group.

  - `ExpiredBidPayment` and `ExpiredOfferPayment` gain `boolean oldRules`.
  - The constructor gains `@NotNull BooleanSupplier holdingAvailable`.
- Lock points:
  1. **`executeBuy`.** Inside the reservation transaction, resolve with `ranBy = null`. A buy never pays agent fees, because no agent ran it. Return the quote in `Success`. No `SaleFee` rows are written yet. Task 7 records them after the money moves, through `recordBuyFees(String regionId, UUID worldId, UUID buyerId, Party seller, FeeQuote quote)`, which writes the lines with status `paid` and `completedAt = NOW()`.
  2. **`clearExpiredBiddingAuctions`, winner branch.**
     - If `!holdingAvailable.getAsBoolean()`, roll back, leave the auction, add nothing to the results and warn once per auction id (decision 7).
     - Otherwise resolve with `ranBy = auction.auctioneerId()`, insert the payment with `totalDue = quote.buyerTotal()`, and `lockFees("bid", bidId, ...)`.
  3. **`clearExpiredBidPayments`.** After `insertNextPayment`, read the new row with `selectByRegion`, and `deleteUnpaidBySale("bid", oldBidId)`. Then resolve for the new bidder, update its `totalDue` with a new mapper method `updateTotalDue(int bidId, double totalDue)`, and `lockFees("bid", newBidId, ...)`.
  4. **`acceptOffer`.**
     - Without a holding account, return `NoHoldingAccount`.
     - Otherwise insert the payment with `totalDue` and `acceptedBy = ctx.player()`. Resolve with `ranBy = ctx.player()`, then `lockFees("offer", offerId, ...)`.
     - `insertPayment` on both mappers gains `double totalDue` and, for offers, `@Nullable UUID acceptedBy`.
- `payBid` and `payOffer` compare against `payment.totalDue()` instead of the price.
- `finalizeBidPurchase` and `finalizeOfferPurchase` check `currentPayment >= totalDue - PAYMENT_EPSILON` and call `markCompleted(kind, saleRef)`. They also record history with the price, as now.
- `clearExpiredBidPayments` and `clearExpiredOfferPayments` call `deleteUnpaidBySale` for the expired payment.
- `deleteRegion` covers decision 5: before deleting, for each payment row with `!oldRules && currentPayment > 0`, insert a `PendingRefund` with reason `region-deleted`, all in the same transaction.

- Produces:

  - `final class SaleSettlement(EconomyProvider economy, RealtyBackend backend, HoldingAccount holding, RealtyEventDispatch events, Logger log)`:
    - `void payOutDue()`. It processes `backend.duePayouts()` one line at a time:
      1. Claim the line with `claimPayout(id)`, skipping it when the claim returns false.
      2. Transfer from the holding party to the recipient, with the ledger message `"Sale payout (" + source + "): " + region`.
      3. On success, call `markPayoutPaid(id)` and fire `FeePaidEvent` for a `fee` line.
      4. On failure, call `markPayoutFailed(id, message)`. When `attempts + 1 == 24`, log SEVERE.
    - `boolean refund(UUID buyerId, double amount, String regionName, @Nullable UUID worldId, String reason)` transfers from the holding party to the buyer. A failure records a `PendingRefund` and returns false. A success fires `RefundPaidEvent`.
    - `void retryRefunds()` does the same claim, transfer and mark sequence over `dueRefunds()`.
  - Backend bookkeeping methods, implemented over the Task 4 mappers. The retry interval is 1 hour and the maximum is 24 attempts, both constants in `RealtyBackendImpl`:
    - `List<DuePayout> duePayouts()`, where `record DuePayout(int id, String lineKind, String source, Party recipient, double amount, String regionName, @Nullable UUID worldId, UUID buyerId, int attempts)`
    - `boolean claimPayout(int id)`, `markPayoutPaid(int id)`, `markPayoutFailed(int id, String error)`
    - `recordPendingRefund(...)`, `List<DueRefund> dueRefunds()`, `boolean claimRefund(int id)`, `markRefundPaid(int id)`, which deletes the row, and `markRefundFailed(int id, String error)`
- Money flow in `RealtyPaperApiImpl`:
  - **Instalment, new rules.** The transfer goes from the buyer to `holding.party().orElseThrow()`. The partial ledger messages stay the same. When the holding party is missing during payment, return `PaymentFailed("no holding account")` and roll back.
  - **Instalment, old rules** (`FullyPaid.oldRules()`, or for `Success` the backend's new `boolean oldRules` component). It pays the seller as today.
  - **Final instalment, new rules.**
    1. The buyer pays the holding party.
    2. `finalize*Purchase`.
    3. The world changes as now.
    4. `settlement.payOutDue()` runs on `dbExec`. Ownership doesn't wait for it.
    5. Fire `AuctionWonPurchaseEvent` or `OfferPurchaseCompletedEvent` with the quote, and return `FullyPaid` with the quote.
  - **Buy** (decision 6). Check the balance against `fees.buyerTotal()`. Then pay the seller `sellerReceives` and each line's recipient its amount, in order. On a failure, reverse the completed transfers in reverse order and roll back the reservation. On success, call `recordBuyFees`.
- Expiry in `Realty.scheduleTasks`:
  - For each `ExpiredBidPayment` or `ExpiredOfferPayment` with `!oldRules && refundAmount > 0`, call `settlement.refund(...)`.
  - Pass the result as `refundPaid` to the expiry event. Old rules or nothing paid count as `false` with a zero amount, as today.
  - Then call `settlement.payOutDue()` and `settlement.retryRefunds()` every run. Both are cheap when nothing is due.
